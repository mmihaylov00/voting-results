# Geography and polling context

These are local snapshots imported by `npm run import:context`, for the eight parliamentary elections in our catalog. Every payload records its upstream URL, SHA-256 and retrieval time. Polling payloads also preserve the upstream calculation timestamp and hashes of the agency registry and publication catalog. No runtime requests are made to the upstream service.

- **Top electoral districts:** calculated in the app from our existing CIK district summaries. The ranking and share denominator exclude district 32 (abroad). Machine share uses machine plus paper ballots. Historical changes compare domestic vote shares with the previous archived parliamentary election.
- **Demographic correlations:** [Наясно's published artifacts](https://storage.googleapis.com/data-electionsbg-com/2026_04_19/dashboard/demographic_cleavages.json), produced by its [municipal correlation pipeline](https://github.com/atanasster/electionsbg/blob/main/scripts/parties/build_demographics.ts) from CIK election data and [NSI Census 2021](https://census2021.bg/), census date 2021-09-07. Values are Pearson correlations across municipalities, not individual voter characteristics or causal effects. We retain the selected parties and coefficients, but omit the upstream `pctNational` field, whose domestic aggregation differs from our national totals. `eduSecondary` includes tertiary education; `religionNoneOrUndecl` is rendered as no religion, matching the producer's actual numerator rather than its field name.
- **Polling agencies:** [accuracy](https://storage.googleapis.com/data-electionsbg-com/polls/accuracy.json), [agency registry](https://storage.googleapis.com/data-electionsbg-com/polls/agencies.json) and [poll publications](https://storage.googleapis.com/data-electionsbg-com/polls/polls.json) from Наясно. The [scoring pipeline](https://github.com/atanasster/electionsbg/blob/main/scripts/polls/analyze_accuracy.ts) selects the last fieldwork end on or before each election. Publications originate from agency websites, press reports and Wikipedia compilations; each displayed poll links to its particular publication. The pipeline proportionally redistributes undecided/non-response shares for raw-attitude polls where available. Both published and adjusted values remain in our snapshots. The comparison retains the election-result version used by that upstream calculation.
- **Where the votes came from:** national matrices from [the transition index](https://storage.googleapis.com/data-electionsbg-com/transitions/index.json), estimated by [Наясно's vote-flow pipeline](https://github.com/atanasster/electionsbg/tree/main/scripts/voteFlows). This is non-negative Goodman ecological regression over matched polling sections, followed by RAS balancing, not observed individual voting or exit-poll evidence. Source pairs are consecutive elections in that corpus; the earlier date can lie outside our archive. Matched and dropped section counts, source hashes and model residuals travel with each snapshot. Published small-edge suppression and rounding mean shown flows need not equal node labels. Some older estimates also have incomplete balancing: the April 2021 pair's source category for other parties exceeds its label by about 8.66%, although its weighted total residual is about 0.123%. We preserve these discrepancies, expose them in the information dialog and calculate displayed origin percentages from the shown incoming flows. The diagram's bands use those same flows, and counts are marked as estimates.

We recompute MAE from unique matched parties. The upstream October 2024 Sova Harris comparison contains two identical BSP rows. Our importer collapses that duplicate and records `deduplicated: 1`; conflicting duplicates cause the import to fail. MAE excludes unmatched or unreported parties, so the UI shows the number compared. A missing party, sample size or historical baseline is not displayed as zero.

Data and calculations are attributed to their original publishers and to [Наясно / Atanas Stoyanov](https://github.com/atanasster/electionsbg).
